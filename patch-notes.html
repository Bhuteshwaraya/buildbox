0.1.7 Clearer replays

  • Restored recorded buffs, debuffs and cooldowns in fight replays.
  • Clarified Ember spending, refunds, Cinder gains and free Detonates.
  • Improved timeline sizing and range zoom; removed the duplicate report timeline.
  • Smoothed startup and simplified fight settings, with five-minute defaults.

0.1.5 A steadier Builder

  • Edit talents inside the Builder and press Enter to save. Starting a run requires a selected talent build.
  • Stat previews and bonus messages keep their place; stat locks and comparison values are clearer.
  • Gem comparisons use exact legal tiers, with clearer lock states and Hidden Riches limits.
  • Character sockets, weapon traits and pane headings are easier to read.
  • Tap Escape once to leave fullscreen. Exit confirms that settings stay saved.

BuildBox checks while open. Choose Update & restart when you're ready. Versions 0.1.2 and earlier need the current installer once to repair updating.
